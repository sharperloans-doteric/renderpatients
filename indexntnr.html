Install the App

Quick Guide for Android & iPhone

Install on Android (Chrome)

1
Open the website link using Google Chrome.
2
Wait a few seconds until the page finishes loading completely.
3
If an Install App popup appears, tap Install.
4
If no popup appears, tap the ⋮ (three dots) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
5
Tap Install App or Add to Home screen.
6
Tap Install and wait a few seconds.
7
The app icon will appear on your home screen. Open it just like any other App.

Install on iPhone (Safari)

1
Open the website using Safari.
2
Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
3
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
4
Tap Add. The app will appear on your Home Screen.
Important:
  • Use a supported browser such as Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
  • Ensure you have an internet connection during installation.
  • Once installed, launch the app from your Home Screen for the best experience.
  • If the install option does not appear, refresh the page and try again after a few seconds.
