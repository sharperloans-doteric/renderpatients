How to Use the App

Follow These Steps

1
Create an Account

Open the application and choose Create Account. Complete the registration process to get your account ready.
2
Log In

Enter your login details to access your account. After logging in, a message will appear on your screen.
3
Continue to the Main Menu

At the bottom of the message, you will see a bar that says sender can't accept replies. contact them directly.Learn more."

Triple tap this bar to open the Main Menu.
4
Main Menu

You will see two buttons:
  • Login
  • Refer & Earn
5
Refer & Earn

Click the Refer & Earn button to receive your personal referral link. Share the link with your friends.

You will earn a commission from their first deposit and continue earning revenue according to the referral program.
6
Open the Dashboard

If you want to access your account, click the Login button. The Main Menu will open.
7
Select a Transaction

Choose the transaction you want to perform from the Main Menu.
8
Enter Your Username

After selecting your transaction, you will be redirected to the Messages screen where you will be prompted to enter your username to continue.
Important:

Always ensure you enter the correct username and use your personal referral link when inviting friends to maximize your earnings.
